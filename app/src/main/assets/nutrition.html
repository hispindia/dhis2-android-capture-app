Nutrition Counseling

  • Take 3 basic food groups in each meal everyday.
  • Eat meat, fish, eggs , milk and milk products everyday.
  • Eat more fresh and organic fruit and vegetables instead of eating preserved food.
  • Reduce eating fatty, salty and sweetened foods.
  • Older people should eat nutritious foods in daily life.
  • Often, older people do not eat enough. An older person can eat more by eating eat family-style meals and social dining, particularly for those living alone or who are socially isolated.
  • Use iodized salt in preparing and cooking food.
  • Drink more water.
  • Reduce taking soft drinks.
  • Exposure to morning sunlight is helpful to make the skin produce vitamin D. The vitamin D in food is not enough for older people to maintain optimal levels.

    • For Diabetic Patients

    • Reduce food intake especially salty, sweety and fatty foods.
    • Eat more foods rich in fiber.
    • Eat lots of fruit and vegetables.
    • Avoid eating fat come from animals - saturated fat (e.g. Chicken skin, duck skin, animals intestine)
    • Eat fat come from fish.
    • Advise all patients to give preference to low glycaemic-index foods (e.g. beans, lentils, oats, unsweetened fruit, tomatoes, dark, green vegetables, salmon, sweet potatoes, raw nuts, etc.).
    • Check and record your blood glucose level regularly.
    • Check your eyes for eye diseases (diabetic retinopathy) at least once a year.
    • Measure the levels of protein in your urine for early detection of renal dysfunction every six months.
    • Check blood cholesterol level every six months.
    • Check blood pressure regularly.
    • Check cardiac function regularly.
    • Avoid walking barefoot or without socks.
    • Wash feet in lukewarm water and dry well especially between the toes.
    • Do not cut calluses or corns, and do not use chemical agents on them.
    • Look at your feet every day and if you see a problem or an injury, go to your health worker.
    • If you are on any DM medication that may cause your blood glucose to go down too low carry sugar or sweets with you
    • Drink more water. If you are thirsty, drink water and avoid drinking soft drinks.
    • Control your body weight and enjoy active lifestyle by following these activities: reduce sitting time, walk as much as you can, use stairs if you can, do household chores, clean and weed your compound and garden.
    • Do regular exercises in your leisure time (30 minutes a day, 5 days a week – a total of 150 minutes each week)
